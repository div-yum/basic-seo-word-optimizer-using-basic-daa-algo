About SEO Word Optimizer

Built as a B.Tech Design & Analysis of Algorithms (DAA) mini-project. It showcases practical applications of core computer science algorithms on user-provided article text without external APIs or black-box libraries.

DAA Algorithmic Scope

  • Binary Search: O(log n) search on sorted unique dictionary.
  • Merge Sort: Guaranteed O(n log n) stable frequency sorting.
  • Quick Sort: Average O(n log n) in-memory partition sorting.
  • LCS (DP): O(m × n) 2D memoized token sequence alignment.

Honest Limitations

Operates strictly locally on user-supplied text. Does not crawl external websites, predict Google SERP rankings, or access proprietary search volume data. All metrics and timings are measured live.
